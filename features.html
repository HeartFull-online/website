Feature Guide

What HeartFull Includes

HeartFull is built around earned trust, not paid access. The features below are part of the product experience: web, Android, and installed web app where supported.

Earned Hearts

Hearts are earned through profile activity, photo/video uploads, replies, likes, referrals, and verified dates. They cannot be bought.

Photo-first messaging

Conversations live on top of the person's photos instead of inside a generic inbox.

Voice messages

Record, send, and play voice messages in chat. Voice is a communication tool, not a connection paywall.

Video calls

Matched users can start 1-on-1 video calls from a conversation using WebRTC.

Dating events

Events support registration, attendee visibility after profile completion, group chat, and event-specific matching.

Event video calls

Online events can use a webinar-style video room with hosts on stage and attendees watching or requesting a guest slot.

Matching and Discovery

HeartFull avoids swipe decks. People appear through matching and photo discovery. Matching uses mutual preferences, completed profiles, and activity signals. Users can turn matching on or off from their profile; when it is on, HeartFull periodically checks for new compatible people instead of reshuffling the feed on every tap.

Profile background imagery is assigned automatically for new accounts through FreeDesignStore lifestyle images. If someone has not posted a gallery yet, their background still gives the profile visual context. Background images are also preloaded in the browsing experience for smoother swiping.

Messaging, Calls, and Safety

The first message to someone new places 1 Heart in escrow. If they reply, that Heart transfers to them. Continued conversation uses demand-aware Heart pricing so low-effort mass messaging becomes expensive while genuine conversations stay natural.

Users can send text, voice messages, photo likes, and 1-on-1 video calls. Calls include microphone, camera, and hang-up controls. Event video calls are separate from 1-on-1 calls and are designed for hosted sessions rather than private dating calls.

Users can block, report, unmatch, mute notifications for a person, and blur another person's photos and profile background from their own feed.

Events and Real Dates

Dating events can be public and include a dedicated event page, registration state, attendee list behavior, group chat, and a default event selection that favors the next upcoming event. If no future event exists, the app can fall back to the last event and its chat.

The QR date check-in tool records an in-person date and awards 100 Hearts to both participants. The system is designed to reward the outcome dating apps should care about: people meeting in real life.

Notifications and Install Options

HeartFull supports push notifications where the platform allows them. Android app users receive native-style app notifications. Web users can use HeartFull in the browser, and installed web app users get the most complete push-notification support. Browser users see notification reminders so they know when installing improves the experience.

You can open the web app directly at heartfull.online or download the Android app from Google Play.

Admin, Moderation, and Promoters

HeartFull includes an internal admin portal for moderation, event operations, user review, promoter operations, and payout reporting. These tools are not dating features for ordinary users, but they keep the platform safer and easier to operate.

Promoters get referral tracking, signup/date/conversation bonus reporting, share links, social flyers, scorecards, signup lists, and payout views. The public promoter program is documented on the Promoters page.

Still true: matching, messaging, visibility, Hearts, voice messages, and calls are not sold as paid access to people. Future paid tools may enhance workflow or assistance, but they do not decide who you can meet.
