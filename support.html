Support

We're here to help.

Contact us

For any questions, issues, or feedback, email us at support@heartfull.online. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app and sign in with your phone number or Apple account. You'll be guided through setting up your profile, including adding photos and basic information.

How do Hearts work?

Hearts are HeartFull's in-app currency. You earn Hearts by uploading photos, receiving likes, having conversations, and going on dates. You spend Hearts to message new people. Your character is your currency.

Is HeartFull really free?

Connections on HeartFull are never paywalled. There are no hidden fees to match with or message other users. Premium tools like AI-assisted features may be offered in the future.

How do I report a user?

Tap on the user's profile and use the report option. All reports are reviewed by our moderation team. We take safety seriously and act on reports promptly.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete account." Your data will be permanently removed. You can also follow the instructions on our account deletion page.

I'm having trouble signing in

Make sure you're using the same phone number or Apple account you originally signed up with. If you're still having trouble, contact us at support@heartfull.online.

How do I change my photos or profile?

Open the app, go to your profile, and tap on any photo to replace it or add new ones. You can update your name and bio from the profile screen.

Does HeartFull support video calls?

Yes. Matched users can start 1-on-1 video calls from a conversation. Dating events can also include online video rooms where hosts go live and attendees can watch or request a guest slot.

Do notifications work on Android and web?

Android app users can receive app notifications. Web users can use HeartFull in the browser, but installing the web app to the home screen gives the most complete push-notification support on platforms that allow it.

What are the lifestyle backgrounds on profiles?

New profiles can receive a safe lifestyle background image automatically, so an account still has visual context before the person posts a full gallery. If you blur someone's photos, their background is blurred too.

Safety

Your safety matters. Read our safety standards and privacy policy. If you encounter inappropriate content or behaviour, report it immediately through the app or email support@heartfull.online.
