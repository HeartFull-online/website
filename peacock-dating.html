Peacock Dating format

Meet people live, at your own pace

A hosted online dating event in HeartFull: watch live interviews, ask questions, or take a turn on stage. Check each event listing for its age range, audience, time and availability. Peacock Dating format is the format name; HeartFull is the company.

Participation is your choice

Everyone can watch, ask questions through chat, queue to speak, or apply to host, regardless of gender. You join the audience with your camera and microphone off. Send a question for a host to read out, or use emoji reactions. Hosting requires staff approval.

The stage and “Go next” queue

Up to two hosts and one rotating guest share the stage: at most three people on camera. Tap “Go next” to join the presenter queue. The first person in line takes the next guest turn; “Pass” leaves the queue. You can stay in the audience for the whole event.

Audience voice — coming soon

Voice from the audience is being added. When available, you can explicitly turn on your microphone without joining the stage; your camera stays off. Hosts can disable audience voice, mute or remove a speaker, and allow one to four audience microphones (three by default). If all slots are full, wait and try again.

Privacy and visibility

Staying off camera does not make your account invisible. You can opt out of the attendee profile list using the event’s roster visibility setting; this also affects whether you appear in the picks list. Eligible completed registrants see only first name, photo, age and a short bio. Blocked pairs and deleted accounts are excluded. In Peacock-format events, women’s attendee profiles are shown only to women; men’s and non-binary members’ profiles can be shown to everyone eligible. These profile-list rules do not restrict who can speak or host. Speaking on stage, using voice or sending chat shares what you choose with the room. HeartFull has no event recording feature.

Connections and private picks

Completing event registration connects you with other completed attendees, subject to account and safety checks. After the event is marked completed, the app offers private picks from the visible attendee list. Once someone has presented, audience members pick presenters; presenters can pick anyone in their visible list. A pick introduction requires both people to choose each other and at least one to have presented. If nobody presented, mutual picks can connect any eligible pair. Registration connections are separate from these mutual-pick introductions; joining does not mean someone picked you.

Costs and Hearts

There is no Hearts charge in the event registration or call flow. Check the event listing for any organiser’s attendance terms. The first message in a conversation introduced by mutual event picks is free; later messages from the person who started the conversation can cost Hearts. Other new conversations normally start with a one-Heart deposit, released when the other person replies. Replies are free and Hearts cannot be bought.

Safety and moderation

Hosts can mute, remove or ban participants. Respect people’s boundaries and do not share someone else’s personal information. Use the app’s block and report tools if needed, or ask a host for help. You can leave whenever you want.

What you need and how to join

Use a phone, tablet or computer that runs HeartFull, a stable internet connection and speakers or headphones. A microphone is needed if you speak; a camera is optional for watching. Headphones help avoid feedback when using voice. Sign in to HeartFull and complete your event registration and profile. A Meetup RSVP alone does not register you in the app. Events run inside HeartFull. If the event is full, you may be waitlisted.
