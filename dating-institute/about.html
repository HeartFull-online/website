About the Dating Institute

Who we are, why we exist, how we handle our bias, and why we publish everything for free.

What this is

The Dating Institute is an independent research initiative studying how the dating app industry affects society. It was founded by HeartFull — a dating app startup building an alternative to the pay-to-win model.

We publish our research freely for anyone to use: competitors, investors, journalists, researchers, and users. Nothing is behind a paywall. Nothing requires a signup. Everything is sourced.

Why it exists

The most prominent dating research — the Kinsey Institute's annual Singles in America study — is funded by Match Group, the company that makes $3.5 billion per year from the industry it studies. Their findings consistently suggest the apps are working well.

We believe the industry that extracts billions from human connection shouldn't be the only one studying whether it's working. Someone should look at the SEC filings, the fertility data, the user satisfaction surveys, and the pricing structures — and say out loud what the numbers actually show.

Our bias — disclosed

We're transparent: HeartFull is a competitor to the apps we analyze. We have a financial interest in the status quo changing. We want people to question whether the current model serves them.

We disclose this upfront because we believe honest bias is better than hidden bias. Match Group funds research that says Match Group apps are great. We fund research that questions the whole model. Both have agendas — we just tell you ours.

Judge our data on its merits. Every claim is sourced. We publish our methodology. And we maintain a Hard Questions page where we critique our own model.

Editorial standards

  • We cite primary sources: SEC filings, World Bank data, peer-reviewed research, app store listings
  • We distinguish between data (facts we can verify) and analysis (our interpretation of those facts)
  • We label editorial opinion explicitly
  • We correct errors publicly
  • We include a Hard Questions page where we challenge our own assumptions

What we publish

  • Industry Analysis (3 pages) — Industry Critique, Population Crisis, Revenue vs Fertility
  • App Directory & Pricing (3 pages) — App Directory, Pricing Comparison, Reputation Data
  • Country Research (13 pages across 6 countries) — USA, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea
  • Behavioral Science — Psychology of Swiping, Privacy Crisis
  • Self-Critique (1 page) — Hard Questions
  • Annual Statistics Report — published each January with updated data

How to use this research

Journalists: cite freely with attribution to "Dating Institute (datinginstitute.heartfull.online)." All data is sourced; no permission needed.

Researchers: every data source is linked directly. Methodology is described alongside analysis. If you want the raw data behind any chart, ask us.

Competitors: use this data to build better products. Seriously. If Bumble or Hinge uses our research to treat users better, that's a win. The point is better outcomes, not market share.

Users: make informed decisions about which apps deserve your time and money. Know what you're paying for, what the success rates actually look like, and what alternatives exist.
