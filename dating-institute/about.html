About the Dating Institute

Who we are, why we exist, how we handle our bias, and why we publish everything for free.

What this is

The Dating Institute is an independent research initiative studying how the dating app industry affects society. Founded by HeartFull, we publish 84 pages of free research covering 17 dating apps, 10 countries, dating advice, community workshops, and the societal impact of how people meet.

Everything is free. Nothing is behind a paywall. Nothing requires a signup. Every claim is sourced. And we maintain a Hard Questions page where we critique our own model.

Our mission

To understand and improve how humans form relationships in the digital age.

Dating apps are now the #1 way couples meet. Over a quarter of marriages start on one. When the primary way people find partners is broken by misaligned incentives — charging money for human connection — it affects families, communities, and society at scale.

We research the problem, publish the data, educate people on dating skills, and build community — because fixing how humanity connects is bigger than any single app.

Our vision

A world where dating platforms are aligned with user success, not optimized against it. Where your ability to meet someone isn't determined by your income. Where the technology that connects people makes them happier, not lonelier.

Why it exists

The most prominent dating research — the Kinsey Institute's annual Singles in America study — is funded by Match Group, the company that makes $3.5 billion per year from the industry it studies.

We believe the industry that extracts billions from human connection shouldn't be the only one studying whether it's working. Someone should look at the SEC filings, the fertility data, the user satisfaction surveys, and the pricing structures — and say what the numbers actually show.

Our bias — disclosed

We're transparent: HeartFull is a competitor to the apps we analyze. We have a financial interest in the status quo changing. We want people to question whether the current model serves them.

We disclose this upfront because we believe honest bias is better than hidden bias. Match Group funds research that says Match Group apps are great. We fund research that questions the whole model. Both have agendas — we just tell you ours.

Judge our data on its merits. Every claim is sourced. We publish our methodology. And we maintain a Hard Questions page where we critique our own model.

Editorial standards

  • We cite primary sources: SEC filings, World Bank data, peer-reviewed research, app store listings
  • We distinguish between data (facts we can verify) and analysis (our interpretation of those facts)
  • We label editorial opinion explicitly
  • We correct errors publicly
  • We include a Hard Questions page where we challenge our own assumptions

What we publish

84 pages of free research across 6 categories:

  • Industry Research (6 pages) — Annual Statistics, Revenue vs Fertility, Industry Critique, Population Crisis, Psychology of Swiping, Privacy Crisis
  • App Intelligence (4 pages) — Global App Landscape, 17-App Directory, Pricing Comparison, Reputation Data
  • Country Research (50 pages across 10 countries) — Deep dives, app landscapes, dating culture, local pricing, safety & regulations for USA, China, India, Japan, UK, Germany, France, South Korea, Brazil, Australia
  • Dating Advice (15 pages) — Research-backed guides for everyone, for men, and for women. Profiles, first dates, messaging, rejection, safety, standards.
  • Community — Free workshops and meetups on ethical, intentional dating
  • Self-Critique — Hard Questions where we challenge our own model

How we give back

The Dating Institute is our way of giving back to the community we serve. Everything we publish is free because we believe the dating industry has taken enough from people — charging for research about the problem would make us part of it.

  • Free research: 84 pages of sourced data, analysis, and advice — no paywall, no signup, no email gate
  • Free workshops: In-person and online dating skills workshops — profile building, first dates, ethical dating
  • Open to competitors: We explicitly invite Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, and every other app to use our research. If it helps them treat users better, that's a win.
  • For journalists and researchers: Cite anything freely. No permission needed. We want this data in the public discourse.

How to use this research

Journalists: cite freely with attribution to "Dating Institute (datinginstitute.heartfull.online)." All data is sourced; no permission needed.

Researchers: every data source is linked directly. Methodology is described alongside analysis. If you want the raw data behind any chart, ask us.

Competitors: use this data to build better products. Seriously. If Bumble or Hinge uses our research to treat users better, that's a win. The point is better outcomes, not market share.

Users: make informed decisions about which apps deserve your time and money. Know what you're paying for, what the success rates actually look like, and what alternatives exist.
