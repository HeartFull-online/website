Key patterns across markets

Tinder dominates the West but not the East. In China (Momo), Japan (Pairs), South Korea (Amanda), and India (Shaadi), local apps that understand the culture outperform global platforms.

Two companies own 70% of the Western market. Match Group and Bumble Inc control the vast majority outside of Asia. This duopoly sets pricing and product standards globally.
