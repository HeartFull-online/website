Dating Institute Community

Free workshops and meetups on ethical, intentional dating. Not a singles event — a skills workshop. Learn to date better, not just more.

What this is

The Dating Institute Community is a free, in-person and online workshop series where people learn the skills that dating apps don't teach: how to build a profile that attracts the right people, how to nurture matches into real connections, how to approach first dates with confidence, and how to date ethically in a world designed to keep you swiping.

This is not a singles mixer. This is not speed dating. This is a skills workshop — like a cooking class, but for human connection. You'll leave with practical tools, not phone numbers.

Why this exists

78% of dating app users report emotional exhaustion. People aren't failing at dating because they're unlovable — they're failing because nobody taught them the skills. Schools don't teach it. Apps profit from you NOT learning it. And most "dating advice" online is either manipulative tactics or recycled platitudes.

We believe dating is a skill that can be learned, practiced, and improved — just like cooking, public speaking, or fitness. The Dating Institute Community is where that learning happens.

Workshop topics

Profile Workshop: Show Your Real Self

Beginner 90 min

Bring your phone. We'll review your dating profile live (anonymously, if you prefer) and rebuild it using research-backed techniques. Which photos actually work. How to write a bio that attracts compatible people, not just anyone. How prompts can filter for what you want.

The Art of the First Message

Beginner 60 min

Why "hey" gets a 25% response rate and profile-specific questions get 62%. Practice writing opening messages that start real conversations. Peer review in small groups. Walk away with 10 ready-to-use openers tailored to your style.

From Match to Date: Nurturing Connections

Intermediate 90 min

Most matches die in the first 48 hours. Learn the rhythm of good conversation — when to text, how much to share, when to suggest meeting up, and how to keep momentum without being overwhelming. Real examples, role-play exercises, and the science of reciprocal interest.

First Date Masterclass

Intermediate 2 hours

Planning, conversation, body language, and follow-up. What research says about first impressions, the questions that build genuine connection (based on Aron's 36 Questions), how to read and send signals, and the art of the second-date suggestion. Practice in a safe, supportive environment.

Ethical Dating in the App Era

Values 90 min

How to date with integrity when the apps incentivize the opposite. Ghosting — why we do it and how to stop. Honesty about intentions. Treating matches as humans, not options. Setting boundaries that respect both people. The difference between having standards and being a checklist machine.

Rejection, Resilience & Self-Worth

Wellbeing 90 min

Dating apps can wreck your self-esteem if you let them. This workshop covers the psychology of rejection (most of it isn't personal), how to protect your mental health while dating, when to take breaks, and building a sense of self-worth that doesn't depend on match counts. Open discussion welcome.

Red Flags & Green Flags

Safety 60 min

Spotting manipulation patterns before they escalate. Love bombing, breadcrumbing, future faking — what they look like in practice and why smart people fall for them. Plus the green flags that actually predict healthy relationships (based on Gottman Institute research).

Dating Across Cultures

Special 90 min

Dating norms vary wildly across cultures. What's romantic in France is forward in Japan. What's normal in Brazil is intense in Germany. This workshop explores cross-cultural dating with respect — based on our 10-country research. Essential for anyone dating in a multicultural city.

How it works

  • Free. Always. No upsells, no premium tiers, no pitch at the end.
  • Small groups. 10–20 people per session. Enough to practice, small enough to feel safe.
  • Mixed format. Some sessions online (Zoom), some in-person (locations TBA).
  • Research-backed. Every technique taught is grounded in psychology research, not guru wisdom.
  • Safe space. No judgment, no pressure, no hitting on other attendees. This is a learning environment.
  • All welcome. Any gender, any orientation, any experience level. Single or in a relationship wanting to improve.
