Dating App Prices in France (2026)

France has a unique dating app landscape. While Tinder leads in downloads, Meetic (owned by Match Group) is the locally dominant platform for serious relationships, with pricing and features tailored specifically to the French market. Happn, founded in Paris, also has an outsized presence. Prices below are in euros with USD equivalents at approximately 1 EUR = 1.08 USD.

How France differs from the US

Meetic is king for serious dating. While Match.com is a secondary brand in the US, its European counterpart Meetic dominates the French serious-dating segment. Meetic's pricing model includes optional add-ons (ZEN for scam protection at 6.99/mo, Premium profile boost at 9.99/mo) that create a layered cost structure not seen in American apps. Monthly costs can quietly climb above 40 when add-ons stack.

Tinder prices skew lower. French Tinder subscriptions are generally 10–15% cheaper than US equivalents, partly due to purchasing-power adjustments and partly due to stronger competition from local players. Age-based pricing remains: users under 28 pay significantly less.

Happn has home-field advantage. Founded in Paris in 2014, Happn's proximity-based model works especially well in dense French cities. Its pricing is competitive, and the app has stronger brand loyalty in France than in any other market.

Tips for users in France

Watch for promotional periods. Meetic regularly runs promotions (especially around Valentine's Day and la rentree in September) that can drop prices to 4.99/mo. If you are willing to wait, you can save substantially.

Consider the 6-month commitment. Across all apps, the 6-month plan typically cuts per-month cost by 50–65% compared to monthly billing. If you plan to use an app for more than two months, the longer plan almost always saves money.

Free tiers are more generous in France. Badoo's free tier includes messaging and video chat — features locked behind paywalls on most competitors. Happn's free tier lets you see everyone you crossed paths with. Start free, then upgrade only if you hit a wall.
