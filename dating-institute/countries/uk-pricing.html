Dating App Prices in UK (2026)

The UK is Europe's largest dating app market, with Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, and Badoo competing for British singles. Prices are generally higher than the US once you account for VAT (20%) and GBP conversion — British users pay a premium for identical features.

Why UK prices are higher than US

UK dating app prices appear comparable at face value — Tinder Plus is £24.99/month versus $25 in the US. But the pound buys more than the dollar, meaning British users are paying ~25% more in real terms for the same features. The 20% UK VAT is baked into the listed price (US prices exclude sales tax), which accounts for much of the difference. Badoo, owned by Bumble Inc., is the one exception — it is notably cheaper in the UK than in the US, likely due to its European roots and larger British user base.

Age-based pricing in the UK

Tinder, Bumble, and Hinge all use age-based dynamic pricing in the UK. Users under 28 typically see prices 20–40% lower than those over 30. A 2024 investigation by Grazia found that users in their 30s were being shown significantly higher prices for identical Tinder Gold subscriptions. This practice is technically legal in the UK but has drawn criticism from consumer advocates.

iOS vs. Android and web pricing

Apple's 30% commission inflates iOS subscription prices across all apps. In the UK, this is compounded by VAT, making iOS the most expensive way to subscribe. Subscribing via the app's website (e.g., tinder.com, bumble.com) is typically 15–25% cheaper than via the App Store. Google Play pricing falls in between. Badoo explicitly offers lower web pricing.

Tips for getting the best deal

1. Subscribe via the web. Avoid both the App Store markup and the Google Play premium by going directly to the app's website.
2. Badoo is the budget pick. At £5.83/month on a 6-month plan, Badoo Premium is by far the cheapest premium option in the UK.
3. Use longer plans. Tinder's 12-month plan drops to £8.33/month — a 67% savings over the monthly rate.
4. Hinge free tier is generous. 8 likes per day with full messaging is enough for most users. Premium adds convenience, not necessity.
5. Watch for UK-specific promotions. Apps often run discounts around Valentine's Day, New Year, and the "January dating surge."
