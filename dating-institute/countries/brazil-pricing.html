Brazil is the second-largest dating app market in the world by users, behind only the US. Brazilian users are among the most active globally, spending up to 12 hours per week on dating apps. Prices are lower than US rates but carry an additional cost: Brazil levies VAT (ICMS) on digital services, which can add 12–25% to listed prices. How Brazil differs from the US

Digital services tax adds to the sticker price. Brazil's ICMS (state-level VAT) applies to app store purchases and can add 12–25% depending on the state. A subscription listed at R$39.90 might cost R$45–50 after tax. This is built into app store charges but rarely shown in advertised prices. US app store purchases, by contrast, only face state sales tax in some jurisdictions.

Prices are 35–50% cheaper than US equivalents. Tinder Plus at R$27.90/month (~$5.30) versus $14.99 in the US represents a 65% discount. This purchasing-power adjustment makes premium tiers more accessible to Brazilian users, contributing to higher paid-subscriber rates than in many Western markets.

Badoo has exceptional market share. While Badoo is a minor player in the US, it ranks as the #2 dating app in Brazil. Its generous free tier (messaging included) and low-cost day pass (R$10.90) make it the go-to choice for budget-conscious users. The lifetime Premium option at R$399.90 is also popular.

Tips for users in Brazil

Check the final price after tax. App store prices in Brazil may show pre-tax amounts. Check your payment confirmation for the actual charge, which will include ICMS. This is especially relevant for weekly billing plans where small tax additions compound.

Badoo's free tier is hard to beat. If you just want to message people, Badoo lets you do it for free — including video chat. In the US, this would require a paid subscription on most apps. Start with Badoo before paying for anything else.

Happn works exceptionally well in major cities. In dense urban areas like Sao Paulo and Rio de Janeiro, Happn's proximity-based matching shines. The 12-month plan at R$29.90/month is the best value for committed users in these cities.
