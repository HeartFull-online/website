Dating App Prices in Australia (2026)

Australian dating app users consistently pay more than their American counterparts — often 15–30% higher even after currency conversion. This is partly exchange-rate driven, partly because Australia's smaller market supports higher per-user pricing, and partly because apps saw a 9% price increase between January 2025 and February 2026. How Australia differs from the US

Everything costs more. Bumble Premium at A$89.99/month (~US$58.50) compares to US$54.99 in the US. eHarmony at A$49.90/month versus US$35.90. This is not just currency conversion — Australian prices are set higher in real terms. Apps treat Australia as a high-willingness-to-pay market, similar to how software and gaming products are historically more expensive there (the so-called "Australia tax").

eHarmony pushes long commitments. The cheapest eHarmony plan requires a 24-month commitment. That is two years locked in at A$19.90/month, totaling A$477.60 (~US$310) upfront. The 6-month plan at A$49.90/month totals A$299.40 (~US$195). Either way, you are paying hundreds before you even match with someone.

Bumble's weekly billing is aggressive. At A$24.99 per week, Bumble Boost costs roughly A$108/month — making it one of the most expensive dating app subscriptions in Australia. The monthly Bumble Premium plan at A$89.99 is slightly cheaper on a per-week basis. This weekly billing model obscures the true monthly cost.

Tips for users in Australia

Avoid weekly billing plans. The weekly prices for Bumble and Tinder look small (A$24.99, A$8.49) but annualize to far more than monthly or 6-month plans. Always calculate the true monthly cost before subscribing.

Hinge offers the best value. At A$15.49/month on a 6-month plan, Hinge+ is the cheapest premium subscription among the major apps in Australia. It includes unlimited likes and the ability to see who liked you — features that cost 2–3x more on Bumble or Tinder.

Use free tiers strategically. Hinge's free tier (8 likes/day) is more generous than most competitors. Tinder's free tier still lets you match and message. Before committing to any paid plan, spend 2–3 weeks on free tiers to determine which app has the best user base in your area. Premium subscriptions are only worth it if the app already works for you.

Watch for auto-renewal traps. eHarmony auto-renews at the Premium Light monthly rate (A$49.90/month) regardless of what plan you originally chose. Set a calendar reminder to cancel before renewal if you do not want to continue.
