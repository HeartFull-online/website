Dating App Prices in China (2026)

China's dating app market operates in its own ecosystem — Western apps like Tinder and Bumble are marginal players, while homegrown platforms dominate. The standout feature: Chinese dating apps are dramatically cheaper than Western equivalents, often 70–85% less per month. Prices below are in RMB (CNY) with USD equivalents at approximately 1 RMB = $0.14 USD.

How China differs from the US

Prices are a fraction of Western rates. A Momo VIP membership costs 12 RMB/month (~$1.70) — compared to Tinder Plus at $14.99/month in the US. Even the most expensive Chinese matchmaker packages rarely exceed $70, while US equivalents (eHarmony, Match.com) start at $35/month for basic tiers. This reflects both purchasing-power differences and a market where free features are far more generous.

Two distinct market segments. Apps like Momo, Tantan, and Soul target casual dating and social connection for younger users (18–35). Baihe and Zhenai serve the marriage market, often used by parents searching on behalf of their children. The marriage-oriented apps charge more and sometimes offer offline matchmaker services bundled into premium packages.

Coin-based microtransactions dominate. Rather than monthly subscriptions alone, Chinese apps heavily use virtual currency systems. Soul's coin model (1 RMB for 6 coins) lets users pay tiny amounts for specific features — extending a call, sending a gift, unlocking a filter. This makes the entry cost near zero but can add up for heavy users.

Tips for users in China

Start with Momo or Tantan free tiers. Both offer surprisingly robust free experiences compared to Western apps. Messaging is generally free, and the VIP upgrade at 12 RMB/month is so cheap it is almost an impulse purchase if you want extras.

Be cautious with matchmaker packages. Baihe and Zhenai upsell aggressively into offline matchmaker services that can cost thousands of RMB. The base subscription is affordable, but the real revenue comes from high-touch services. Make sure you understand what you are paying for before committing.

Soul is different. Soul matches on personality quizzes and voice, not photos. If you are tired of swipe-based apps, it offers a genuinely different experience — and the coin-based pricing means you can try it for almost nothing.
