Dating App Prices in India (2026)

India's online relationship market splits into two distinct categories: dating apps (Tinder, Bumble) used mainly by urban millennials and Gen Z, and matrimonial platforms (Shaadi, Jeevansathi, BharatMatrimony) used by families seeking marriage matches. The pricing models are fundamentally different. Prices below are in INR with USD equivalents at approximately 1 INR = $0.012 USD.

How India differs from the US

Matrimonial apps use per-contact pricing. Unlike Western subscription models where you pay for time-based access, Indian matrimonial platforms often bundle a fixed number of contact views (e.g., 75 phone numbers with Shaadi Gold). Once you exhaust your contacts, you must upgrade. This creates a fundamentally different cost structure — power users pay far more than casual browsers.

Tinder India is the cheapest Tinder in the world. At Rs 189/month (~$2.27) for Plus, Indian Tinder pricing is roughly 85% cheaper than US rates. This reflects purchasing-power parity adjustments. However, the feature set is identical — Indian users get the same unlimited likes and passport as American users paying $14.99.

BharatMatrimony's Assisted tier is a different product entirely. At Rs 65,000+ (~$780), you are not just buying app access — you are paying for a dedicated human matchmaker who screens candidates, arranges introductions, and manages communication. This blurs the line between app and service.

Tips for users in India

Know which category you need. Dating apps (Tinder, Bumble) are for casual dating and relationship exploration. Matrimonial platforms (Shaadi, BharatMatrimony, Jeevansathi) are for marriage-oriented searches, often involving family. Using the wrong category wastes money and time.

Matrimonial free tiers are limited by design. You can browse profiles for free on all three matrimonial platforms, but you cannot message or view contact details without paying. The free tier is essentially a preview. Budget for at least 3 months of paid access if you are serious.

Tinder and Bumble offer extreme value in India. At under Rs 200/month, Tinder Plus is cheaper than a cup of coffee at most urban cafes. Bumble's daily pass at Rs 90 lets you try premium for a day before committing. Both are worth experimenting with before investing in a matrimonial subscription.
