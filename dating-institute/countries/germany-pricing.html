Dating App Prices in Germany (2026)

Germany's dating market is split between swipe-based apps (Tinder, Bumble, Lovoo) and traditional matchmaking platforms (Parship, ElitePartner). The latter are among the most expensive dating services in the world, with contracts that can lock users in for 12–24 months at €45–75/month.

Parship and ElitePartner: Germany's expensive outliers

Parship and ElitePartner (both owned by ParshipMeet Group) are dramatically more expensive than any US dating app except Tinder Select. A 6-month Parship plan costs roughly €450 ($486) upfront. These platforms position themselves as serious matchmaking services for educated professionals and use detailed personality questionnaires (80+ questions) to generate compatibility scores. In Germany, this model works — Parship is the country's highest-grossing dating platform.

Be aware of the contract terms: Parship and ElitePartner typically require 6-, 12-, or 24-month commitments paid upfront or in installments. German consumer law requires a cancellation notice period (usually 12 weeks before contract end), and these platforms have faced legal action over aggressive auto-renewal practices.

How Germany compares to US prices

For swipe-based apps, Germany is significantly cheaper than the US. Tinder Plus at €10/month (~$11) is less than half the US price of $25. Bumble and Lovoo are also 30–50% cheaper than their US equivalents. However, the matchmaking platforms (Parship, ElitePartner) have no real US equivalent at their price point — eHarmony is the closest comparison but costs roughly half as much. Germany's overall dating spend skews higher due to Parship's dominance in the 30+ demographic.

iOS vs. Android and platform differences

Tinder, Bumble, and Lovoo all cost more on iOS than on Android or web. Parship and ElitePartner are primarily web-based platforms, so app store commissions are less relevant — but you should still subscribe via the website to avoid any markup. German users benefit from strong consumer protection laws: since March 2022, digital subscriptions must offer a one-click cancellation button (the "Kündigungsbutton"), though Parship has been slow to comply fully.

Tips for getting the best deal

1. Tinder and Lovoo are bargains. At €10/month each, they are among the cheapest dating subscriptions in any Western market.
2. Avoid Parship's shortest plan. The 6-month plan at €75/month is the worst value. If you commit to Parship, the 24-month plan halves the monthly cost.
3. Use the Kündigungsbutton. German law requires a cancellation button — use it well before your contract renewal window to avoid being locked into another term.
4. Subscribe via web. Avoid App Store and Google Play markups by going directly to the app's website.
5. Watch for seasonal offers. Parship frequently runs 20–50% off promotions around January and September, when sign-up demand peaks.
