The HeartFull economy

How Hearts work

Hearts are how you connect on HeartFull. You earn them by being a real, active person, and you spend a few to start conversations. You can never buy them — on HeartFull, your character is your currency. Everyone starts with 100 Hearts.

The one-line version: Saying hi costs 1 Heart, and it goes to the other person the moment they reply. After that, each message you send in a normal chat costs about 1 Heart. Replying is always free — and it earns you the Heart they sent.

Earning Hearts

Every way to earn rewards something genuine — showing up, sharing yourself, and actually meeting people. Most earns are capped per day so nobody can farm the system and flood the app.

Notice the last row: when a message costs Hearts, those Hearts don't go to HeartFull — they go to the person you're talking to. The total supply stays finite and meaningful, and the most engaging people naturally accumulate the most.

The first message: a held Heart

When you message someone new, 1 Heart is held aside (not spent yet). The moment they reply, that Heart becomes theirs. It's a small, honest signal that you mean it — and a thank-you to them for engaging. If you unmatch before they ever reply, the held Heart comes straight back to you.

Ongoing messages: a fair, predictable price

After the first hello, each message you send is priced by a simple formula. The person who was approached never pays a thing, and replies are always free.

price = base × how busy their inbox is

The base

How busy their inbox is

Messaging someone whose inbox is already flooded costs a bit more — this protects popular people from being buried, and nudges you to make your message count.

The price is always at least 1 Heart, and never more than you actually have. For the vast majority of conversations — two regular people chatting — it's just 1 Heart per message.

Real-life examples

Maya & Sam, two newcomers

Both joined this week with 100 Hearts. Maya spots Sam and says hi — 1 Heart is held. Sam replies, so that Heart becomes his (he's now at 101, Maya at 99). They get talking. Each follow-up Maya sends costs 1 Heart (similar Hearts, quiet inboxes). Sam pays nothing to reply.

Base 1 × demand ×1 = 1 Heart per message. After 20 messages Maya has spent ~20 Hearts — easily topped up by posting a photo or two.

Priya messages Leo, who's very popular

Priya has 80 Hearts. Leo gets about 15 messages a day, so his inbox is busy. Priya's messages cost a little more — and every Heart she spends goes to Leo.

Base 1 (she's reaching up, so no penalty) × demand ×3 = 3 Hearts per message. She can still send ~26 messages on her balance — the price just nudges her to lead with something real instead of "hey".

Dana, a Heart-rich regular, messages newcomer Tom

Dana has earned 2,000 Hearts over months of activity. Tom just joined with 60. Because Dana is far wealthier, her messages to Tom cost a bit more — and those Hearts lift the newcomer.

Base = 0.5% of 2,000 = 10 (capped) × demand ×1 = 10 Hearts per message, flowing straight to Tom. Dana can still chat freely; she just can't cheaply spray hundreds of new people.

Running low on Hearts?

You're never locked out. Even at 0 Hearts you can always reply to anyone (free — and you collect the Heart they held for you), and you can always earn by posting, getting likes, or going on a real date. Hearts run low only if you're starting lots of conversations and engaging in none.

Why it's built this way

Fair to newcomers

Reaching up never costs a penalty — you pay the floor. Your first 100 Hearts are always enough to start real conversations.

Protects popular people

The busier someone's inbox, the more a message costs — so genuine interest rises to the top instead of getting buried under "hey".

No pay-to-win

Hearts can't be bought at any price. The only way up is genuine effort: posting, replying, and meeting people in real life.

Rewards meeting

The biggest single reward — 50 Hearts each — is for actually going on a date and scanning each other's QR code.

Always true: the person who was approached never pays, replying is always free, and you can never be charged more Hearts than you have. Matching, messaging, visibility, and Hearts are never for sale.
