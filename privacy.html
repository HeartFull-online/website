Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Our commitment

HeartFull is built on the belief that dating apps should respect their users, not exploit them. That applies to your data too. We collect the minimum necessary to run the app, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for behavioral advertising.

What we collect

  • Account information — phone number (for verification), name, age, gender, and preferences you provide
  • Profile content — photos and text you choose to share on your profile
  • Messages — conversations between you and other users, stored to deliver the service
  • Hearts balance — your earned Hearts count and transaction history
  • Device information — device type and browser for compatibility (not for tracking)
  • Usage data — basic analytics to improve the app (aggregated, not individual)

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data to third parties
  • We don't share data with advertising networks
  • We don't use your behavioral patterns for targeted advertising
  • We don't collect biometric data from your photos
  • We don't track your location continuously — only when you explicitly share it

How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Match you with compatible people based on your preferences
  • Deliver messages between you and your matches
  • Calculate and display your Hearts balance
  • Improve the app experience through aggregated, anonymized analytics
  • Prevent fraud, spam, and abuse

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase) infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. We follow industry-standard security practices. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of your data
  • Correction — update inaccurate information
  • Deletion — delete your account and all data
  • Portability — export your data in a standard format

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, hosting
  • Google Fonts — font delivery (no user data shared)

Changes to this policy

We'll notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Minor updates will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at privacy@heartfull.online.
