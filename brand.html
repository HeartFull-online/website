Brand Guidelines

Warm, direct, and unmistakably HeartFull.

HeartFull should feel human before it feels technical. The brand is built around earned trust, real dates, and the promise that connection is never sold to the highest bidder.

Logo

HeartFull

Use

  • Use the coral-to-rose heart gradient whenever possible.
  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least half the heart width.
  • Use warm light surfaces or deep ink backgrounds.

Avoid

  • Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or recolor the heart arbitrarily.
  • Do not place the logo over noisy imagery without a solid contrast layer.
  • Do not use cold blue as the primary brand color.

Color

HeartFull's color system comes from the logo: vivid coral, soft rose, warm neutrals, and deep ink. Blue is reserved for provider-specific or technical states only, not brand expression.

Typography

Fraunces

Use for brand, hero, and major page headings. It gives HeartFull its warmer, less generic voice.

DM Sans

Use for body copy, controls, dense admin screens, labels, forms, and navigation.

Product UI

Primary actions

Use Heart Coral for primary buttons, active states, hearts, and important progress moments.

Surfaces

Use warm cream backgrounds and white elevated surfaces. Avoid sterile white-and-blue layouts.

Shape

Use 12px button radii and 8-12px cards unless a component already has a stronger local convention.

Admin

Admin screens should stay calm and operational, but they still use the HeartFull palette for theme seed and active states.

Voice

Write like a clear person, not a growth funnel. HeartFull copy should be direct, warm, and honest about tradeoffs. Avoid shaming users, implying scarcity tricks, or framing paid features as access to people.

Core line: character over credit card. Connection is earned through behavior, not purchased through advantage.

Imagery

Use real lifestyle and social-context imagery where the person, place, or moment is readable. Profile backgrounds from FreeDesignStore should feel safe, warm, and human, not like generic dark stock photography.

Implementation References

Website

Public pages define the palette with CSS variables such as --coral, --coral-light, and --surface.

Android and web app

The user app uses app/lib/app/theme.dart and app/lib/app/mobile_theme.dart for the coral-led product theme.

Admin portal

The admin portal should seed Material colors from Heart Coral, not default blue.
