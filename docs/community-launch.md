## Workshop Curriculum Outline

### Session 1: Profile Workshop (90 min)
1. **The problem** (10 min) — Why most profiles look identical and what data says about which ones work
2. **Photo review** (30 min) — Bring your photos; we'll score them using research-backed criteria. Peer feedback.
3. **Bio writing** (20 min) — Live editing. Specific > generic. Show don't tell.
4. **Prompts** (15 min) — How prompts filter for compatibility
5. **Q&A** (15 min)

### Session 2: First Message Workshop (60 min)
1. **The data** (10 min) — Response rates by message type (with actual numbers)
2. **Examples** (15 min) — Good vs bad messages. What makes them work.
3. **Practice** (25 min) — Write 5 messages to sample profiles. Peer review.
4. **Q&A** (10 min)

### Session 3: From Match to Date (90 min)
1. **The 48-hour window** (10 min) — Why most matches die and how to keep momentum
2. **Conversation rhythm** (20 min) — When to text, how much, when to suggest meeting
3. **Role play** (30 min) — Practice text conversations in pairs
4. **The ask** (15 min) — How and when to suggest meeting in person
5. **Q&A** (15 min)

### Session 4: First Date Masterclass (2 hours)
1. **Planning** (15 min) — Venue, timing, preparation
2. **Conversation** (30 min) — Questions that build connection (Aron's research)
3. **Body language** (20 min) — Reading and sending signals
4. **Practice** (30 min) — Mock date conversations in pairs
5. **The follow-up** (15 min) — What to text after, when, how to handle "no spark"
6. **Q&A** (10 min)

### Session 5: Ethical Dating (90 min)
1. **The incentive problem** (15 min) — Why apps encourage bad behavior
2. **Ghosting** (20 min) — Why we do it, the cost, how to stop
3. **Honesty** (20 min) — Intentions, exclusivity, difficult conversations
4. **Boundaries** (20 min) — Setting them, respecting others', the difference between standards and walls
5. **Discussion** (15 min)

### Session 6: Rejection & Resilience (90 min)
1. **The numbers** (10 min) — What "rejection" actually means on apps
2. **The psychology** (20 min) — Why it hurts and why most of it isn't personal
3. **Mental health** (20 min) — Warning signs, when to take breaks
4. **Self-worth** (20 min) — Building identity outside of apps
5. **Open discussion** (20 min) — Safe space for sharing experiences

## Tone Guidelines

DO:
- Be warm, honest, and practical
- Acknowledge that dating IS hard — don't minimize it
- Use research and data to back up advice
- Make people feel safe to be vulnerable
- Treat all genders with equal respect

DON'T:
- Use pickup artist language or manipulation tactics
- Promise "results" or "matches"
- Shame anyone for their dating history or choices
- Push HeartFull the app aggressively (mention it, don't sell it)
- Use gendered stereotypes or assumptions
