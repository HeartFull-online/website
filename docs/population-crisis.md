## The Numbers

### Global Fertility Collapse
- **Replacement rate** is 2.1 children per woman. Most developed nations are far below.
- **By 2050**: 75%+ of countries will be below replacement fertility
- **By 2100**: 97% of countries below replacement (Lancet)
- **Global TFR**: ~2.2 in 2026 and falling

## The Loneliness Epidemic

### US Surgeon General's Advisory
In 2023, US Surgeon General Dr. Vivek Murthy issued a formal advisory — "Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation" — declaring loneliness a public health crisis.

**Health effects of loneliness:**
- As deadly as smoking **15 cigarettes a day**
- **29% increased risk** of heart disease
- **32% increased risk** of stroke
- **50% increased risk** of dementia in older adults
- Significant increases in depression and anxiety

**Scale:**
- **30%** of Americans aged 18-34 feel lonely daily or several times a week
- **1 in 5** adults worldwide experiences persistent loneliness (WHO, 2025)
- **$6.7 billion** excess Medicare spending annually from social isolation

## How Dating Apps Contribute

### Making Loneliness Worse
- **78%** of dating app users feel emotionally or physically exhausted (Forbes Health)
- **80%** of college students skip dating apps entirely
- Studies link frequent app use to **higher depression and anxiety**
- More than **half of app users** never go on in-person dates
- Dating apps shifted from facilitating dates to **accumulating matches**
- AI algorithms may be **hindering healthy relationship development** (PMC study)

### Lower Relationship Quality
- Cross-national study (50 countries, ~6,500 people): couples who met online scored **slightly lower in intimacy, passion, and commitment**
- "Participants who met a spouse in online dating had less satisfying and stable marriages" (ScienceDirect)
- Meeting online means less knowledge of each other's social networks, creating trust challenges

### The Cascade to Population Decline
1. Dating apps are the **#1 way couples meet**
2. The apps create **swipe fatigue, loneliness, and burnout**
3. Users either give up on dating or form **lower-quality relationships**
4. Lower relationship satisfaction → **delayed or avoided family formation**
5. Delayed family formation → **fewer children**
6. Fewer children → **population decline**

This is not the only cause of population decline (economics, housing, career priorities all play major roles). But when the primary way people form relationships is broken, it contributes to the problem. **Fixing how people meet is part of fixing the population crisis.**
