# Competitor Pricing — May 2026

All prices are monthly rates. Most apps use dynamic pricing based on age, location, and platform (iOS tends to be higher). Longer plans reduce the per-month cost.

## Tinder

| Plan | 1 month | 6 months | 12 months |
|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| Plus | $24.99/mo | $16.66/mo | — |
| Gold | $39.99/mo | $23.33/mo | — |
| Platinum | $49.99/mo | $29.99/mo | — |

- Weekly plans available: Plus $12.99/wk, Gold $18.99/wk, Platinum $24.99/wk
- Dynamic pricing: users under 28 often see lower prices
- Free tier: limited swipes, can't see who liked you, no boosts

## Bumble

| Plan | 1 month | 3 months | Notes |
|------|---------|----------|-------|
| Boost | $17/mo | — | Unlimited swipes, backtrack, 5 SuperSwipes |
| Premium | $40/mo | — | + Incognito, travel mode, BeeLine, advanced filters |
| Premium+ | $79.99/mo | $53.33/mo | + Priority Likes, daily boosts, Trending Tab |

- Premium+ weekly: $39.99/wk
- One of the most expensive dating subscriptions on the market
- Prices may be higher on iOS due to App Store fees

## Hinge

| Plan | 1 month | 3 months | 6 months |
|------|---------|----------|----------|
| Hinge+ | $29.99 | $59.99 ($20/mo) | $89.99 ($15/mo) |
| HingeX | $49.99 | $99.99 ($33/mo) | $149.99 ($25/mo) |

- HingeX adds: Skip The Line (continuous boosting), priority likes, enhanced algorithm
- Roses (to contact "Standouts") cost extra on top of subscription
- Users over 30 may see higher prices ("age tax")

## OkCupid

| Plan | 1 month | 3 months | 6 months |
|------|---------|----------|----------|
| Basic | $34.99–$44.99 | $23.33/mo | $14.99/mo |
| Premium | $49.99–$54.99 | $28.33–$29.99/mo | $22.49/mo |

- No monthly payment plans — must pay full amount upfront
- Pricing is market-dependent
- Was once completely free; now heavily paywalled

## Grindr

| Plan | 1 month | 3 months | 12 months |
|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| XTRA | $19.99 | $39.99 ($13.33/mo) | $99.99 ($8.33/mo) |
| Unlimited | $39.99 | $79.99 ($26.66/mo) | $239.99 ($20/mo) |

- EDGE (AI tier): $349.99–$499.99/mo (launched 2025/2026)
- Purchased as lump-sum packages, not billed monthly
- Fined 5.8M EUR by Norway for privacy violations

## Badoo

| Plan | 1 day | 1 week | 1 month | 3 months | Lifetime |
|------|-------|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| Premium | $0.79 | $3.99 | $9.99–$12.99 | $31.99 | $79.99 |

- Cheapest major dating app, but still has paywalls
- Pricing varies by country, platform, and promotions
- 1.5-star average rating across review sites

## HeartFull

| Plan | Cost |
|------|------|
| Everything | $0 forever |

- No premium tiers
- No in-app purchases
- No boosts for sale
- No paying to see who liked you
- Hearts (in-app currency) can only be earned, never purchased
