How to bring people in

You don’t need a big audience. Anyone who joins with your link, QR code, or promo code is credited to you automatically. Here are simple ways real promoters do it — pick the ones that fit you.

  1. Invite people you already chat with

    On WhatsApp, Telegram, or Instagram DMs, send your link: “I’m on HeartFull — a free dating app, no paywalls. Join with my code and add me.”

  2. Share your QR code in person

    Show or print your QR at meetups, cafés, campus, or the gym. People scan it and land in the app already linked to you — nothing to type.

  3. Send your link to friends

    One tap shares your personal link in any messenger. They open it, sign up, and you’re credited automatically — no code to type.

  4. Recommend it on other dating apps

    Matched on Tinder, Bumble, or Hinge? Suggest moving to HeartFull (it’s free) and ask them to add you as their promoter with your code when they sign up.

  5. Post on your socials

    A quick story or post — “trying this free dating app” — with your link or QR. Once you’ve passed 10 referrals, approved posts can also earn $5–$20 each.

  6. Bring friends to events

    Invite people to a HeartFull dating event or mixer. Events convert best — everyone who shows up is ready to meet.
