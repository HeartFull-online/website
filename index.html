No app store. No download. Just open and go.

HeartFull works right in your browser. Save it to your home screen and it looks and feels like a native app, with push notifications when someone messages you.

iPhone & iPad

  1. 1. Open app.heartfull.online in Safari
  2. 2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) at the bottom
  3. 3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. 4. Tap Add. Done! HeartFull is on your home screen with notifications.

Android

  1. 1. Open app.heartfull.online in Chrome
  2. 2. Tap the three dots menu (top right)
  3. 3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  4. 4. Tap Install. Done! Full app experience with push notifications.
