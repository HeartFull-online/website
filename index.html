The Hearts Economy

Every dating app has an in-app currency — coins, boosts, super likes. The difference? They sell theirs for cash. Hearts are earned by sharing lifestyle photos and videos, never bought.

This is the cornerstone: payments are completely disconnected from connections. We'll monetize premium tools like AI and voice — never your ability to meet and talk to people.

Earn Hearts

  • ♥ Share your lifestyle — post photos and videos from your real life to earn Hearts.
  • ♥ Show what you are up to — food, places, activities, hobbies, or everyday moments all help people get to know you.
  • ♥ Keep it real — your posts show people the life behind your profile, not just a posed dating photo.
  • ♥ Earn, do not buy — there is no shortcut with money.

Spend Hearts

  • ♥ Attach Hearts to a message — add Hearts when you reach out to show that you mean it.
  • ♥ Message anyone nearby — people are selected by location and criteria, with no mutual swipe required first.
  • ♥ Make your message count — Hearts make it clear that you put thought into saying hello.
  • ♥ Let them decide — the person you message chooses whether they want to reply.

Why people prefer this

♥

Jerks get filtered out

Sharing your real life gives people context before they start a conversation.

♥

Hearts = trust signal

Hearts come from sharing, not spending money. They show effort, not income.

♥

Conversations matter

When reaching out costs Hearts, people actually think about what to say. Messages are thoughtful, not mass-produced pickup lines.

♥

Income doesn't matter

A student with great character has the same chances as a CEO. Your dating life is determined by who you are, not what you earn.
